## The Why-How-Who Framework

Each pattern is structured around three questions:

| Dimension | Question | Covers |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Why** | What is this for? | Purpose, outcomes, use cases, contraindications |
| **How** | How does it work? | Phases, timing, intervention style, artifacts |
| **Who** | Who is involved? | Roles, group size, requirements, power dynamics |
