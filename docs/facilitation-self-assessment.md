# Facilitation Self-Assessment: During the Session

A self-assessment checklist for facilitators to evaluate their competencies during live sessions.

*Source: Lucy Chambers, [Facilitation Self Assessment v0.3](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1u99QOQDOqTWZ9dlL8sFcc0Rokr9Tt4B6wOB6RhTnOwk/edit)*

---

## Setup & Alignment

- [ ] I ensure the group is aligned on context & purpose for the session
- [ ] I work well with co-facilitators

## Trust & Safety

- [ ] I create and sustain an atmosphere of appropriate trust and safety to meet the session objectives
- [ ] I build rapport with participants

## Communication

- [ ] I give clear instructions and communicate well
- [ ] I practice active listening and observing without judgement
- [ ] I clarify observations by stating what I observe as a facilitator and inquiring into different views
- [ ] I encourage others to practice active listening, curiosity and observing without judgement
- [ ] I promote mutual understanding among group members by clarifying and summarising

## Participation & Inclusion

- [ ] I support and encourage participants with different styles, needs and ways of processing information to share their perspectives
- [ ] I serve the needs of the group as a whole
- [ ] I spot barriers to participation and have ways to address them
- [ ] I create opportunities for participants to understand and benefit from the diversity of the group
- [ ] I cultivate cultural awareness and sensitivity

## Intervention & Feedback

- [ ] I know when to intervene, when not to, and how to provide feedback to participants
- [ ] I can navigate a range of behaviours demonstrated by group members

## Conflict & Tension

- [ ] I recognise the value of tension and conflict in group processes
- [ ] I provide a safe environment for conflict to surface
- [ ] I encourage critical or dissenting perspectives while keeping the atmosphere constructive

## Thinking & Ideation

- [ ] I can help individuals identify and review underlying assumptions
- [ ] I can encourage creative and divergent thinking
- [ ] When there are too many ideas (e.g. after a brainstorm), I have effective methods of allowing the group to narrow them down

## Group Dynamics & Energy

- [ ] I effectively manage group energy (both energising & grounding)
- [ ] I identify information the group needs, and help them surface it
- [ ] I help build self-awareness and process literacy in the group by helping the group spot patterns in their discussion and behaviours

## Process Management

- [ ] I help groups move forward by identifying and capturing appropriate actions and checking commitment
- [ ] I can assess and communicate group progress to help participants follow the process
- [ ] I adapt processes, my style and pace to changing situations and needs of the group
- [ ] I recognise and clarify tangents and have ways to refocus the group on the task
- [ ] I understand that the word "consensus" is understood differently in different contexts. I clarify the appropriate meaning and can guide the group to consensus.

## Closure & Reflection

- [ ] I deliver sessions successfully
- [ ] I help the group reflect on its experience
- [ ] I gather feedback at appropriate intervals and adjust my approach accordingly
- [ ] I recognise that some negative feedback is a sign I am supporting an effective transformation

---

## How to Use This Assessment

1. **Before a session**: Review competencies relevant to the upcoming session
2. **After a session**: Rate yourself on each competency (e.g., 1-5 scale or Yes/Developing/No)
3. **Periodically**: Identify 2-3 areas to focus on improving
4. **With peers**: Use as a framework for peer feedback and co-facilitator debriefs
